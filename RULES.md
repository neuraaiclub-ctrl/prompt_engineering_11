# NEURA Prompt Engineering Hackathon
## Official Rules and Regulations

Welcome to the NEURA Prompt Engineering Hackathon! To ensure a fair, competitive, and secure environment for all participants, all teams must adhere to the following rules and regulations. By logging into the Arena, you and your team agree to these terms.

---

### 1. General Conduct
- **Collaboration**: You may collaborate strictly within your registered team of 1-4 members. 
- **Professionalism**: Maintain professional and respectful behavior. Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language will result in immediate disqualification.
- **Integrity**: The objective is to demonstrate human prompt engineering skills. Attempting to bypass the scoring system, attacking the judging LLM (e.g., prompt injection attacks), or disrupting the platform for other participants is strictly prohibited.

### 2. The Arena Platform Rules
The competition takes place within the proprietary NEURA Arena. The following technical constraints are strictly enforced:

- **Fullscreen Requirement**: The Arena must be completed in Fullscreen mode. Once the countdown finishes, exiting fullscreen will trigger an aggressive overlay blocking your workspace until you return.
- **No External Assistance**: You must write prompts organically. Switching tabs, leaving the window, or using external tools (including ChatGPT, Claude, or other LLMs) during an active challenge is prohibited.
- **No Copy-Pasting**: The use of copy and paste (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V) inside the challenge editor is disabled. Pasting pre-written text is not allowed. 
- **Time Limits**: Once you lock a prompt, the timer is recorded. Submissions made after a challenge timer expires may be rejected or heavily penalized.

### 3. Anti-Cheat Monitoring & Telemetry
The NEURA Arena employs aggressive anti-cheat telemetry to maintain the integrity of the competition:
- The platform tracks and logs every instance of tab-switching, window blurring, and exiting fullscreen.
- A **Red Security Banner** will permanently display on your screen if a violation is detected.
- All telemetry is visible to the judges in real-time on the Judge Dashboard.

### 4. Judging and Scoring
- Submissions are evaluated by our automated LLM judge across 5 strict dimensions: Clarity, Specificity, Context, Output Format, and Constraints.
- **Zero Tolerance on Plagiarism**: If a submitted prompt is identical or functionally identical to the original "broken" prompt, or clearly copied from another team, it will receive an automatic zero across all dimensions.
- All scoring decisions made by the automated system and human adjudicators are final.

### 5. Rights Reserved by NEURA
- **Right to Disqualify**: NEURA reserves the right to eliminate any team at any time for violating these rules, attempting to cheat, or accumulating excessive security flags. **Disqualifications are final and not subject to appeal.**
- **Right to Modify**: We reserve the right to modify the challenges, extend time limits, or restart the server in the event of unforeseen technical issues.
- **Data Usage**: By participating, you grant NEURA the right to store, analyze, and anonymously publish your prompt submissions for educational and platform-improvement purposes.

---

*Good luck, and may the best prompt engineers win!*
